Gupi Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Gupi is a private, single-user assistant. Its only user is its owner, who built it. It is not offered to the public, and it collects no data from anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google sign-in, Gupi may access the owner's own Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Drive data, limited to the permissions the owner approves on the consent screen.

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out requests the owner makes to the assistant, such as reading or editing calendar events or finding a message or file. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with other people.

Where it is stored

Sign-in credentials are stored on a computer owned and controlled by the owner. Google data is read when the owner asks for it and is not copied into any public service. When the owner asks the assistant something, the relevant text may be processed by the AI model service that powers the assistant, solely to answer that request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Gupi's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can revoke Gupi's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete stored credentials from his own computer.

Contact

Questions can be raised as an issue at github.com/mrdeeds89/gupi-assistant.
