Gupi

Gupi is a personal home assistant built and used by one person, its owner, for his own calendar, notes and smart-home devices.

It is not a public product or service. There is nothing to sign up for, and no one other than its owner uses it or is asked to sign in to it.

It connects to the owner's own Google account (Calendar, Gmail and Drive) so that the owner can ask it to look up and manage his own information.
